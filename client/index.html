Eagle Creek Golf Club Highlights

Par and Difficulty

Par 72 (Front 9: Par 36, Back 9: Par 36)

Course Rating: 71.4

Course Slope: 113

Challenging for experts, enjoyable for intermediates, and welcoming for beginners.

Yardage

7,198 yards from the championship tees

Tee Box Options

Championship: 7,198 yards Regular: 6,720 yards Forward: 5,942 yards Beginner: 5,152 yards

Public Course

No stay required

Cart Recommended

Walking allowed

Course Style

Parkland

Notable Features

Tree-lined fairways, undulating greens, and strategically placed bunkers. Water hazards come into play on several holes.

Hole Championship Course

Designed for all skill levels with challenging layouts.

Tee Time Flexibility

Convenient booking options to suit your schedule.

Architect and History

Designed by renowned architect Ron Garl, the course has hosted regional tournaments.

Luxury Clubhouse

Relax with premium dining, locker rooms, and event spaces.

Golf Lessons Available

Expert instruction for players of all levels.

Pro Shop

Fully stocked with top-tier gear, apparel, and accessories.

SPECIAL PACKAGES

Stay & Play packages include two rounds of golf , a cart and accommodations at a nearby resort starting at $250 a person

Eagle Creek Course Feature

Practice Facilities

  • Driving range with target greens
  • Putting green
  • Short game area with sand bunkers
  • Professional lessons available

Amenities and Services

Caddies and Golf Cart Rentals
  • Availability: Golf carts are equipped with GPS and available for rental. Caddie services are available upon request.
  • Costs: Cart rentals: $25; Caddie fees: $40/hour.
On-Course Food and Beverage
  • Multiple snack bars and a halfway house are conveniently located. Beverage carts circulate regularly.

When is the Best Time of Year to Play at Eagle Creek Golf Club?

Fall and Spring are the best seasons to play at Eagle Creek Golf Club. During these months, the weather is mild with minimal rain, providing perfect golfing conditions. Winter is also a great option for those seeking year-round play in Florida’s warmer climate.
